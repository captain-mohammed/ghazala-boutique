## Brand Identity
- **Name (Arabic):** بوتيك غزالة
- **Name (English):** GHAZALA BOUTIQUE
- **Meaning:** "Ghazala" = gazelle — chosen for elegance and grace
- **Tagline (Arabic):** أناقة تمشي بخطى واثقة ("Elegance that walks with confidence")
- **Positioning:** Soft, elegant, graceful — NOT bold/trendy/youthful, NOT luxury/expensive. Budget-friendly-to-mid, feels premium.
